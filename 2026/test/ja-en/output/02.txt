1. A drain discharge inspection device used for an indoor unit of an air conditioner including a drain pump capable of discharging water inside a drain pan and a float switch that switches between an ON state and an OFF state according to a water level in the drain pan, the device comprising:
a battery housing portion capable of detachably holding at least one battery;
a battery connector portion to which a mobile battery can be electrically connected;
a voltage conversion portion configured to convert an output voltage of the at least one battery held in the battery housing portion and an output voltage of the mobile battery connected to the battery connector portion into a driving voltage for the drain pump;
a pump connector portion to which the drain pump can be electrically connected and which is electrically connected to the voltage conversion portion;
a pump drive switch configured to electrically connect the voltage conversion portion and the pump connector portion in the ON state, and to electrically disconnect the voltage conversion portion and the pump connector portion in the OFF state;
a switch connector portion to which the float switch can be electrically connected and which is electrically connected to the battery housing portion and the battery connector portion; and
a first notification portion capable of notifying a state of the float switch,
wherein, when the drain pump is connected to the pump connector portion and the pump drive switch is in the ON state, an output voltage of the at least one battery or an output voltage of the mobile battery is supplied to the drain pump through the voltage conversion portion, and
when the float switch is connected to the switch connector portion and the float switch is in the ON state, an output voltage of the at least one battery or an output voltage of the mobile battery is supplied to the float switch.
