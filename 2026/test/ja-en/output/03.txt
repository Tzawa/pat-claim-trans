[Claim 1]
Drain water discharge inspection device used for indoor unit of air conditioner having drain pump capable of discharging water inside drain pan and float switch switched between ON state and OFF state according to water level inside drain pan, wherein a drain pump is electrically connectable to: a battery housing portion capable of detachably holding at least one battery; a battery connector portion to which a mobile battery is electrically connectable; and a voltage conversion portion capable of converting an output voltage of the at least one battery held in the battery housing portion and an output voltage of the mobile battery connected to the battery connector portion into a drive voltage of the drain pump, the float switch may be electrically connected to a pump connector portion electrically connected to the voltage conversion portion, a pump drive switch electrically connecting the voltage conversion portion and the pump connector portion in an ON state and electrically disconnecting the voltage conversion portion and the pump connector portion in an OFF state, a switch connector portion electrically connected to the battery housing portion and the battery connector portion, and a first notification portion capable of notifying a state of the float switch, when the drain pump is connected to the pump connector portion and the pump drive switch is in an ON state, the output voltage of the at least one battery or the output voltage of the mobile battery is supplied to the drain pump via the voltage conversion portion, and when the float switch is connected to the switch connector portion and the float switch is in an ON state, the float switch is connected to the float switch, an output voltage of the at least one battery or an output voltage of the mobile battery is provided.
