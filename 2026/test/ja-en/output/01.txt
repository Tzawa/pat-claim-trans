A drain discharge inspection apparatus for use with an indoor unit of an air conditioner that includes a drain pump configured to discharge water from inside a drain pan and a float switch configured to switch between an ON state and an OFF state according to a water level in the drain pan, the apparatus comprising: a battery housing unit configured to detachably hold at least one battery; a battery connector unit to which a mobile battery is electrically connectable; a voltage conversion unit configured to convert an output voltage of the at least one battery held in the battery housing unit and an output voltage of the mobile battery connected to the battery connector unit into a drive voltage for the drain pump; a pump connector unit to which the drain pump is electrically connectable and that is electrically connected to the voltage conversion unit; a pump drive switch configured to electrically connect the voltage conversion unit and the pump connector unit in an ON state and to electrically disconnect the voltage conversion unit and the pump connector unit in an OFF state; a switch connector unit to which the float switch is electrically connectable and that is electrically connected to the battery housing unit and the battery connector unit; and a first notification unit configured to indicate a state of the float switch; wherein when the drain pump is connected to the pump connector unit and the pump drive switch is in the ON state, at least one of: (i) an output voltage of the at least one battery; and (ii) an output voltage of the mobile battery, is supplied via the voltage conversion unit to the drain pump; and when the float switch is connected to the switch connector unit and the float switch is in the ON state, at least one of: (i) an output voltage of the at least one battery; and (ii) an output voltage of the mobile battery, is supplied to the float switch.
